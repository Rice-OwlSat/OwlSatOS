# OwlSat Hardware Block Diagram — Plain-Text Summary

**Source:** `OwlSat Hardware block diagram.png` (repo root).
**Purpose:** A text transcription of the drawing, so firmware work can be done, reviewed and
diffed without opening the image. The image governs. When the two disagree, the image is right
and this file is stale — fix this file.

Everything here is *drawn*, not *built*. Part numbers followed by `?` are the diagram's own
question marks and are not settled.

## Domains

The drawing is divided into five boxes: **Sensor Domain**, **Core**, **Radio domain**,
**High Power Stuff**, and **Solar Charging**, plus an unboxed battery/heater group.

---

## 1. Sensor Domain

### Science chain (the EUV instrument)

```
5x EUV photodiodes ──5x coax──> MMCX connectors? ──> Multiplexer ──> Transimpedance Amp ──> ADC ──I2C0──> Flight Computer
                     (analog)                        ^                ^
                                                     |                |
                                                MUXSEL (3x)      GAINSEL (2x)   [red, from FC]
```

- **Photocurrent is estimated at ~1.3 nA** for most sun spectra, as measured by the LISIRD
  TIMED SEE SSI instrument. This is the number that sizes the whole front end.
- **ADC options:** ADS7828 (from AMSAT). 8-channel 12-bit I2C SAR with an internal 2.5 V
  reference. It sits on **I2C0**, not SPI.
- The **transimpedance amplifier gain is switchable** for higher dynamic range — two select
  lines, so four gain steps.
- The **multiplexer needs low leakage**: at these currents its leakage competes with the signal.
- One mux and one amplifier serve all five diodes. The array is therefore sequential: every
  reading costs a channel change plus settling.

### Power to the sensor domain

```
(rail) ──> Power Monitor (ADM1176?) ──> Power switch (dual low power) ──┬──3.3v──> Science
                    |                           ^                      └──3.3v──> Attitude
                  I2C0                     SENS_PWR (x2)  [red, from FC]
```

Both halves of the sensor domain are power-gated, and the monitor is on I2C0 with everything
else. With SENS_PWR low, the ADC does not answer on the bus at all.

### Attitude

- 6 DOF IMU — LSM6DSOTR or BMI088.
- 3 DOF magnetometer — LSM303AGRTR.
- Both on I2C0, both fed by the second channel of the dual power switch.

### Open questions on the drawing

- MMCX connectors for the coax runs — not settled (`MMCX connectors?`).

## 2. Core

### Flight Computer

- **RP2350** + complementary parts.
- **2 Mb code MRAM + 2 Mb data MRAM** — the diagram notes a preference for splitting code and
  data MRAM so that data cannot overwrite code. Sizing note: 8 Mbit (1 MB) for code and
  2 Mbit (256 KB) for data is considered good.
- 4x neopixel for debug, 1 debug LED, boot / reset / user buttons.

### Watchdog

- `WDT_WDI` from the FC, `VBUS_RESET`, and a `Vwatchedrail` output back into the Power block.
- **Note on the drawing:** the WDT_WDI pulse train is generated by a *task*, not a timer. A
  timer may keep running while the CPU is wedged; task switching requires a working CPU, so a
  task-driven kick actually proves something.

### Power block

- Main 3.3 V buck.
- RF LDO → `RFvdd`. Open question on the drawing: *does the LDO have enough capacity?*
- Magnetorquer PSU (integrates inrush protection and OCP) → `MAG_Vdd`, enabled by `MAG_EN`,
  status back on `STAT`.
- USB-C charging ← USB-C connector; `USB D+/D−` to the FC.

### RTC

- 32.768 kHz crystal, backup battery, battery charger. On I2C, 3.3 V.

## 3. Battery, heater and power monitoring

- **Battery:** 2 cells + thermistor. 2S, so **cell balancing is required**.
- **Resistive heater** through a **Heater Switch**, driven by `HEAT` (red) from the FC.
  `THERM` (analog) returns cell temperature.
- **ADM1176 power monitor** on the battery: `BAT_SENS` analog, `I2C0`, and a `Gate` output.
  `Vbatt` is distributed from here.
- The diagram notes inhibit circuitry still needs adding.

---

## 4. Solar Charging

- **2x LTC4121** chargers: one fed by **3x solar panels**, one by **2x solar panels**.
- Each charger reports `FAULT+CHRG` (red) to the FC and a `SOLAR_I_n` current (green, analog).
- **2x (1/2) ADC** — two halves of an ADC — read the panels, on I2C0, 3.3 V.
- **Cells:** Spectrolab XTE-SF, MPP 2.435 V, so 3 in series ≈ 7.4 V.
- Each panel has a Schottky diode so a generating panel's current cannot be dragged through a
  non-generating one.

## 5. Radio domain

```
GPS Antenna ──RF──> RF Stuff?? ──RF──> GPS ──I2C0──> FC
                                        ^
              Power switch ──> Power Monitor (ADM1176?) ──> GPS
                    ^                    |
               GPS_PWR (red)            I2C0
               RFvdd (power in)

RFvdd ──> Power switch ──> Power Monitor (ADM1176?) ──> AMSAT LTM-1 ──RF──> Telemetry/Science antennae
              ^                          |
         RADIO_PWR (red)                I2C0

FC ──SPI0/CAN_CS──> CAN Transceiver (SPI <-> CAN) ──CAN──> AMSAT LTM-1
```

- Both radio loads are individually power-switched and current-monitored.
- The LTM-1 is reached over CAN, bridged from SPI0 by a transceiver — it is not on the
  micro's buses directly.

### Open questions on the drawing

- Splitter/combiner or LNA for GPS? Most CubeSats use one.
- AMSAT LTM-1: power draw (0.32 W in SAFE mode at 7.7 % duty), 5 V supply, whether it can act
  as a cross-band repeater for ham radio while transmitting telemetry, and what protocol it
  speaks (AX.25 appears to be the standard).

## 6. High Power Stuff

### Burn wires (antenna deployment)

- `BURN_X` (multiple pins, red) from the FC → **burn wire switches** → **burn wires**.
- Supplied from `VBATT`. The diagram questions the usual practice of running burn wires from
  Vsolar: battery voltage is much more reliable, especially with little solar power available.
- **Initial deployment must set a key in NVM** recording that the antennae have already been
  deployed, so the burn wires are never triggered a second time.

### Magnetorquer

- **MAX22200**, 4x full bridge → protection diodes → magnetorquer coils.
- Supplied from `MAG_Vdd`; monitored by an ADM1176? on I2C0.
- Control lines: `MAG_EN`, `MAG_CS`, `MAG_CMD`, `MAG_FAULT`, over SPI0.
- 0.4 W in each direction at 3.3 V; the coils will handle up to 6 V.
- **Warning on the drawing:** PWM modulation is a possibility, but the magnetorquer must
  remain off unless it is definitely wanted on.
- Open question: can `MAG_FAULT` be queried from the µC over SPI?

## 7. Buses at a glance

| Bus | Occupants |
|---|---|
| **I2C0** | EUV ADC (ADS7828), sensor-domain power monitor, IMU, magnetometer, battery ADM1176, solar ADCs, GPS, radio power monitors, magnetorquer power monitor |
| **SPI0** | CAN transceiver (`CAN_CS`), magnetorquer (`MAG_CS`) |
| **UART0** | GPS |
| **CAN** | AMSAT LTM-1, via the SPI↔CAN transceiver |

I2C0 is heavily shared. Any driver that talks on it is sharing with the power monitors that
report the health of its own rail, so bus arbitration is a board-level concern, not a
per-driver one.

## 9. Unresolved items carried on the drawing

Transcribed as written, because firmware assumptions should not quietly outrun them:

- Research a microcontroller to use as ADC and GPIO for the solar section; TI has inexpensive
  AEC-Q100 rated parts.
- Decide whether measuring panel voltages is worth it, and how best to do it.
- Evaluate the solar panel configuration — more cells per face if new panels are affordable;
  all panels in series would be simpler at the cost of a higher voltage drop.
- Decide battery chemistry. UL-approved cells are needed to avoid in-house testing.
- Design the power switches and power monitors: eFuse IC or discrete? Which current monitor?
  Is there an inexpensive part that does both?
- Research eFuse functionality.
- ADM1176: does it function as a fuel gauge? How do the current limit and trip features work?
  Should the standard power switch be used here instead?
- Evaluate inrush limiters — look at NyanSat's implementation; possibly build a combined
  inrush limiter and current trip.
- Verify that short / SEL detection works on everything.
- Decide MRAM size and configuration.
- Mark I2C addresses and address-select pin selections on the diagram.
- Put boot/reset in the USB-C connection?
- Look at how to completely disconnect the USB-C charging system once in flight.
